About

The artist behind DeeDiva.

Beauty is a craft, and a diva knows every detail matters — that's the standard behind every face, gele, and look DeeDiva touches.

Jennifer Ndieze

Founder & Lead Artist, DeeDiva Beauty

DeeDiva Beauty began with a simple belief: that beauty work is performance-grade craft, not an afterthought. What started with makeup brushes and a passion for transformation has grown into a full beauty and styling practice — covering everyday glam, bridal parties, gele, nails, and full SFX character work for film and stage.

Every booking gets the same standard, whether it's a bride's morning-of glam or a production that needs a character transformed in front of the camera. That range — soft bridal beauty one day, full prosthetic SFX the next — is what DeeDiva is known for.

Behind the name is a working artist who treats every client, performer, and production the same way: with precision, patience, and an eye for the detail that makes a look actually hold up — through tears, heat, stage lights, or a twelve-hour shoot day.

What guides the work

Gele that holds, nails that last, makeup that survives tears, heat, and twelve-hour shoot days without a touch-up emergency.

Diva energy, on purpose — every client should leave feeling like the most put-together version of themselves.
